ABOUT

[ NMLZE is a cutthroat of brazilian-born producer Joao Antonio Bezerra, born from a lifelong obsession on dubstep and EDM music since his childhood and early teenage years. what started in his bedroom with a cheap PC with less storage and no equipment became a full-time obsession on his musical journey, adopting the former "NORMAL!ZE" alias on his discography until his 3-year musical hiatus.

With every release, the 19-year old producer consists the dark-themed IDM and ambient music with the slaugther of slam metal and the aggression of tearout dubstep and riddim, fueling with ignorance, chaos and distortion on his sound design. NMLZE isn't just about the well-common "angry music" - it's about having a passion on leading a ferocity on the modern bass music too. ]
