ABOUT

NMLZE is a cutthroat of brazilian-born producer Joao Antonio Bezerra, born from a lifelong obsession on dubstep music since his childhood and early teenage years. what started in his bedroom with a cheap PC with less storage and no equipment became a full-time obsession on his musical journey, adopting the former "NORMAL!ZE" alias on his discography until his 3-year hiatus.

With every release, the 19-year old producer consists the dark-themed ambient music with the slaugther of slam metal and the aggression of tearout, fueling with ignorance, chaos and distortion on his sound design. NMLZE isn't just about the well-common "angry music" - it's about leading a ferocity on the modern bass music too.
