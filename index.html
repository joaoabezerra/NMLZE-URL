ABOUT

brazilian-born producer joao antonio bezerra - aka NMLZE (pronounced as "normalize"), has pushed all of his limits into his musical carrer with his chaotic and heavy sound design. since his teenager years, joao started his connections with the dubstep community, using limited equipment that he had in his room and his lightweight PC, releasing his first-ever track using a free-test version of his DAW, and causing him to evolve in his sound design over the years, adopting a balance of classic and modern brostep until his musical carrer hiatus, using the "NORMAL!ZE" alias.

as a 19 year-old man, NMLZE is planning to reopen his musical journey, infusing all of the slaughter, horror, chaos and aggression elements of deathstep, minatory, tearout and slam metal on his sound design, focusing on both dark rooms and hostile crowds on the bass music scene.
